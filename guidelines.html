📋 CCS Virtual Office Guidelines

Addendum to the CCHRSO Online Work Operations. In addition to the University's Protocols for Online Work Operations, the College implements the following procedures to provide continuous service and accessible communication channels for its stakeholders and the Lasallian community.

Virtual Office Setup Queue & Guest Management Admin & Faculty Consultations Staff Attendance Communication Channels Revision History

🖥️ Virtual Office Setup

  • Centralized Virtual Office: the College maintains a centralized Zoom session to serve as its virtual office during work hours, seamlessly supporting both online and onsite work arrangements. Standard hours of operation are 8AM–5PM, Monday to Friday, excluding holidays and work suspensions.
  • Breakout Rooms: managed by the CCS Technical Support Group (TSG), the Zoom session features pre-defined breakout rooms dedicated to specific office units and functions.
Office of the Dean Office of the Associate Dean Department of Computer Technology (DCT) Department of Information Technology (DIT) Department of Software Technology (DST) AdRIC ADRAS & ADEALM (shared) Technical Support Group Faculty Consultations CCS Administrator Consultations Waiting Room

Regardless of work arrangement (online or onsite), TSG is responsible for operating the Zoom session from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM and providing immediate technical support.

🎫 Virtual Queue and Guest Management

To maintain an orderly flow of transactions, the following step-by-step workflow is implemented:

1Entry and Registration

  • Waiting Room: guests entering the Zoom session are held in the Waiting Room, which serves as the virtual queue.
  • Virtual Queue Registration: to gain entry to the main session, guests must complete the Virtual Queue Registration Form, providing their name, ID number, degree program, office needed to contact, and a brief description of their concern.
  • Registration Reminder: if a guest joins the Waiting Room but fails to complete the form, the Front Desk Officer of the Day (FOD) must send a broadcast message to the Waiting Room participants reminding them to register before admission can be granted.

Hello and welcome to the DLSU CCS Virtual Office!
To help us address your concern promptly, please register first by filling out this form: https://tinyurl.com/CCSVirtualOfficeQueue (link on the displayed QR code).
Once submitted, you will be placed in a virtual queue, which you can view at https://tinyurl.com/CCSVirtualQueueStatus, and a CCS support staff member will be assigned to assist you shortly.
Thank you for your patience.

2Queue Assignment and Status Tracking

  • Virtual Queue Number: upon submitting the form, the guest automatically receives a unique Queue Number at the email address they provided during registration.
  • Virtual Queue Status: the Virtual Queue Status displays the list of guests currently being attended to and still waiting to be assigned. The staff assigned to an inquiry must promptly update the call status so waiting guests are kept informed of their place in the queue.

3Reception and Routing

  • The designated FOD welcomes the guest in the Main Room, verifies their inquiry based on their registration form, and assigns them to the breakout room with the appropriate or available staff who can assist them.
  • Status Update: immediately upon assigning the guest, the FOD must update the Virtual Office Monitoring, set the Call Status to Assigned, and log the name of the staff assigned to handle the inquiry.
  • If necessary, the FOD should politely inform guests regarding expected delays during high-traffic periods.

4Service Evaluation

Following the resolution of an inquiry, the assigned staff member must request the guest to accomplish the Virtual Office Feedback Form. The feedback results will be used for continuous service improvement and optimization, as well as staff performance recognition.

5Resolution and Monitoring

Once the consultation concludes, the assigned staff member must promptly update the Virtual Office Monitoring with the following details:

  • Call Status: change to End.
  • Concern Status — one of:
    • New: the inquiry has just been logged but not yet processed (primarily for initial queueing).
    • In Progress: the inquiry is ongoing and resolution is still pending.

      Use this status for internal CCS consultations. If the concern is to be referred to the CCS APO or another CCS staff, keep the status as In Progress and tag the other staff in the "Transferred to CCS APO or another CCS staff" column.

    • Resolved (CCS): the inquiry has been fully addressed and completed by the respective CCS unit.
    • Transferred: select only if the guest is referred to another University office or unit outside CCS that is better equipped to handle the request. If selected, specify the non-CCS unit or office in the "Referred to Another Office" column.
  • Referral: if the concern was referred to another University office or unit outside CCS, tag the request with the concerned office or unit in the "Referred to Another Office" column.
  • Resolution: staff must provide detailed descriptions of how the inquiry was handled or to whom it was referred — for documentation, to inform other staff of how an inquiry was handled, to build the knowledge base, and to record transaction history per guest or inquiry.

🎓 CCS Administrators or Faculty Consultations

For scheduled consultations with CCS administrators

  • The TSG will review the CCS Administrators' Virtual Consultation Schedules daily and prepare dedicated breakout rooms accordingly.
  • Guests with pre-scheduled administrator consultations must undergo the standard reception, registration, and queueing procedure before the FOD assigns them to their designated breakout room.

For scheduled consultations with CCS faculty

  • The TSG will prepare these dedicated faculty breakout rooms as needed.

👥 Staff Attendance and Participation

All CCS support staff are expected to be present and active during the session in accordance with their approved work schedules. If a staff member must step away or is unavailable due to official work commitments or approved leaves, they must coordinate with the designated FOD to make necessary adjustments and ensure continuity of service.

To facilitate proper monitoring of the required 8-hour workday while on WFH, all activities that require staff to be off the virtual office must be recorded in the "Activities outside Virtual Office while on WFH" section on the CCS Staff Schedules sheet (2nd tab), including the time, duration, and purpose/nature of the activity.

FOD assignments by work arrangement

🏠 Full Online

Work Arrangement All CCS support staff are working from home (notwithstanding the work arrangements of laboratory technicians).
FOD Arrangement FOD and Backup: assigned on a rotational basis across all CCS units.
Virtual Office Attendance All CCS support staff must be logged into and active in the Virtual Office.

🔀 Flexible (FWA)

Work Arrangement CCS support staff alternate between onsite reporting and WFH based on approved schedules.
FOD Arrangement FOD (Online): rotated among staff on their scheduled WFH day.
FOD (Onsite): rotated among staff reporting onsite.
Virtual Office Attendance At least one (1) support staff member per unit must be present in the Virtual Office.
  • If a unit staff member is on WFH, they must be logged into and active in the Virtual Office.
  • If no unit staff are on WFH: Academic Departments → Office Manager; Other Units → staff assigned on an alternating basis.
  • If there is only one (1) unit staff, they are automatically assigned to the Virtual Office.

🏢 Full Onsite

Work Arrangement All CCS support staff are physically reporting to the campus.
FOD Arrangement FOD: assigned on a rotational basis across all CCS units.
Virtual Office Attendance At least one (1) support staff member per unit must be present in the Virtual Office.
  • Academic Departments → Office Manager.
  • Other Units → staff assigned on an alternating basis.
  • If there is only one (1) unit staff, they are automatically assigned to the Virtual Office.

Attendance tracking

  • TSG will generate the Zoom attendance report at the end of each day and upload it to the provided CCS Attendance Reports folder.
  • Submission of the reports to the CCHRSO will be done by the Unit Assistants following review and endorsement by their respective Unit Heads.

📧 Supplementary Communication Channels

Email response protocols

It is important to reassure our stakeholders that we remain fully operational and ready to assist them during office hours despite the online work arrangement.

  • To help communicate this clearly, standard templates have been provided for your email replies (Email Templates).
  • All incoming email inquiries must be addressed within 24 to 48 hours of receipt. At a minimum, a formal acknowledgment email must be sent within this timeframe if an issue requires more time to complete.

Physical document submission

To facilitate physical document submissions during online work operations, the following protocol is enforced:

  • Designated drop boxes are available at the CCS Administrative Offices windows, where clients may submit documents at their convenience.
  • Submissions must be placed in a sealed envelope and clearly labeled with the submitter's name and department.
  • Support staff will send an email confirmation acknowledging receipt of the documents on the next scheduled on-site day.
